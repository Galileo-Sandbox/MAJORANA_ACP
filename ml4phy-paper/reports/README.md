# Paper reports

Reports are the human-readable audit trail. They explain why work was run,
what was reused, how a protocol changed, what completed, and which claims the
evidence does or does not support.

## Recommended reading path

1. `artifact_release_v1.md`: public artifact scope and validation.
2. `data_budget_ledger.md`: unique data accounting and classifier dependency.
3. `gap_decision.md` and `extension_gap_decision.md`: audit-before-training
   decisions.
4. `phase1_extension_handoff.md`, `phase2_result.md`, and `phase3_result.md`:
   campaign outcomes.
5. `paper_presentation_export.md` and `paper_coverage_export.md`: definitions
   behind the main portable tables and figures.
6. `mc_smoothness_result.md`: numerical diagnostic and its claim boundary.

Planning, pilot, approval, and recovery reports are retained because they
document prospective choices and failures. Later reports may supersede an
earlier proposed action, but they do not erase it. Mechanism-control reports
live with their additive implementation under `../review_followup_20260910/`.
